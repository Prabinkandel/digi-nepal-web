ABOUT US

Digital tools, a little closer to home.

Digi Nepal brings popular software and subscriptions together in one place — with local payment options, clear pricing, and a team that's here to help.

Our Mission

We started Digi Nepal because getting digital subscriptions in Nepal shouldn't be complicated. Whether it's AI tools for work, design software for your next project, or entertainment for your downtime — we make it simple to find, order, and manage everything from one place.

Every product on our platform comes with transparent pricing in NPR, local payment methods like eSewa and Khalti, and order tracking from your personal account.

🎯

Clear Pricing

Every subscription shows its full price in NPR upfront. No hidden fees, no surprises at checkout.

🔒

Secure Access

Protected accounts with session management, optional two-step verification, and encrypted credentials.

💬

Local Support

Get help through WhatsApp, email, or your account dashboard. Real people, real responses.

Why Digi Nepal?

Access to global digital tools shouldn't depend on having an international credit card. We bridge that gap by offering a trusted local platform where you can browse subscriptions, pay through familiar methods, and track your orders — all in one place.

Our team manually verifies every payment and processes orders with care. We believe in doing things right, not just fast.
